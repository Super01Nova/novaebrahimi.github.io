I am a PhD candidate in the <a href="https://cci.charlotte.edu" target="_blank">College of Computing and Informatics</a> at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, working across UX research, AI, and immersive technology. My research studies how people learn and collaborate in desktop virtual worlds, and turns what experts and users know into design guidance that teams can act on. I developed the Socio-Spatial Embodiment (SSE) framework, which identifies the spatial, social, and interface strategies that make virtual learning spaces work. I combine qualitative, quantitative, and AI-assisted computational methods, and I am currently building an AI co-designer that gives educators adaptive design guidance while keeping them in control of the design. I also teach and mentor student design teams in HCI and interaction design courses, guiding them from research insights to design decisions.
